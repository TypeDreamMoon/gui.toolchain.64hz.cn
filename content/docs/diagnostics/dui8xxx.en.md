---
title: "DUI8xxx · Layout"
description: "A compiled screen, laid out at a viewport size, shows a problem. From DreamMCP's layout and preview only."
---

## DUI8001

`LayoutOffScreen`

What can show of a node reaches past an edge of the viewport, or lies wholly outside it.

## DUI8002

`LayoutTextOverflow`

A text cut short by its overflow policy, or laid out past its box.

## DUI8003

`LayoutTooSmall`

A node that draws or can be pressed of zero size, or a pressable control under 24 x 24 px.

## DUI8004

`LayoutOverlappingControls`

Two pressable controls of one canvas on top of each other.

## DUI8005

`LayoutNearlyAligned`

Siblings whose edges, centres or gaps miss lining up by 1 to 3 px (an info).

## DUI8006

`LayoutBreaksAtSize`

A layout problem at one of the further viewport sizes asked for, and not at the first.

## DUI8007

`LayoutLowContrast`

A text that stands out from what is behind it less than 3 to 1 (preview only).
