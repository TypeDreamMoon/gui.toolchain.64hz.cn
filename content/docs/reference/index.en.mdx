---
title: Class reference
description: Every public DreamGUI class's properties, functions and events, printed from reflection, with its UMG comparison.
---

One page per class: its properties (type, category, whether the details panel shows it, how a Blueprint reads and writes it), the functions it offers, its events, and at the end how its names compare with the UMG class it corresponds to. The pages are printed from reflection by the plugin's `DreamGUIReferenceDocs` commandlet, so they cannot fall behind the headers:

```powershell
UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe <project>.uproject -run=DreamGUIReferenceDocs
```

Why the names follow UMG's, and which members differ on purpose: [UMG parity](/en/docs/controls/umg-parity).
